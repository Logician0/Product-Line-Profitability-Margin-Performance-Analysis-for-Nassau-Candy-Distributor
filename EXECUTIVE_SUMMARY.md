# Executive Summary: Nassau Candy Product Margin Audit

**To:** Leadership Team & Board of Directors  
**From:** Commercial Analytics  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Scope:** 10,194 Transactions across FY2024–FY2025  

---

### The Big Picture

Over the last 24 months, Nassau Candy booked **$141,783 in revenue** and **$93,443 in gross profit** (a **65.9% blended gross margin** across 38,654 units shipped). While the top line looks solid, our product-by-product audit uncovered three major strategic risks and one immediate profit opportunity:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| FY2024-2025 FINANCIAL HEALTH CHECK                                                      |
+--------------------------+-------------------+------------------------------------------+
| Total Invoiced Sales     | $141,783.63       | 10,194 customer orders                   |
| Total Cost of Goods      | $48,340.83        | 34.1% aggregate COGS ratio               |

| 80% Profit Concentration | 5 of 15 SKUs      | Top 33% of catalog generates 95% margin  |

+--------------------------+-------------------+------------------------------------------+
```

---

### Three Core Findings

1. **Extreme Concentration in Chocolate:**
   The Chocolate division drives **92.9% of company revenue and 95.1% of gross profit**. The five Wonka Bar lines are our core money-makers (averaging 65–71% gross margin). However, this leaves Nassau Candy vulnerable to cocoa price shocks and facility downtime at Hicksville and Livonia.

2. **Severe Margin Leakage in Kazookles:**
   *Kazookles* generated $1,205 in sales but only **$92.75 in gross profit** due to a **92.3% supplier cost ratio** ($3.00 cost on a $3.25 selling price). After warehouse picking, packing, and billing overhead, *every box of Kazookles loses money*.

3. **Untapped High-Margin Gems in Sugar:**
   *Everlasting Gobstoppers* (80% margin, $8.00 unit profit) and *Hair Toffee* (77.8% margin, $3.50 unit profit) are our most profitable items per unit, but they received virtually zero sales focus ($427 total Sugar division sales over 2 years).

---

### Four Immediate Steps for Q4

| Priority | Action Item | Financial Impact | Timeline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Urgent** | **Reprice & Renegotiate Kazookles:** Cut vendor COGS by 20% or raise wholesale price from $3.25 to $3.75. | **+$420 / year profit recovery** | 30 Days |
| **2. High** | **+3.5% Price Hike on Top 3 Wonka Bars:** Inelastic demand allows +3.5% price increase with $<1\%$ volume impact. | **+$2,850 / year cash gain** | 60 Days |
| **3. Growth** | **Bundle Gobstoppers with Chocolate:** Cross-sell high-margin Gobstoppers (80% margin) in B2B chocolate orders. | **+$3,200 / year margin expansion** | 90 Days |
| **4. Hygiene** | **Delist Fun Dip & Nerds:** Drop negative-ROI tail SKUs to free up warehouse slotting and picking time. | **+$650 / year labor savings** | Q1 2027 |

**Total Estimated Bottom-Line Profit Gain: +$7,120 / year (+7.6% profit expansion).**
