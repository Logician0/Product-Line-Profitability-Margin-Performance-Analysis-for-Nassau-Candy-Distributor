**Target Audience:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO), VP of Commercial Sales, VP of Supply Chain

### Executive Briefing

An exhaustive financial audit of **10,194 customer transactions** totaling **$141,783.63 in sales revenue** and **$93,442.80 in gross profit** (blended gross margin of **65.91%**) reveals critical strategic insights regarding Nassau Candy Distributor’s product portfolio:

1. **Top-Line Sales Mask Critical Margin Leaks:** While overall enterprise margin appears healthy at 65.91%, individual product gross margins range from **80.00%** (*Everlasting Gobstopper*) down to an alarming **7.69%** (*Kazookles*).
2. **Severe Profit Concentration in Chocolate Division:** The **Chocolate Division generates 95.06% of total company gross profit** ($88,824.62) from 92.88% of sales. The top 5 Wonka Bar SKUs carry the entire company's bottom-line performance.
3. **Acute Margin Drain in 'Other' Products:** *Kazookles* accounts for $1,205.75 in sales but generates only $92.75 in gross profit due to an unsustainable **92.31% COGS ratio** (Unit Price: $3.25, Unit Cost: $3.00). When accounting for order fulfillment and warehousing overhead, *Kazookles is destroying shareholder value*.
4. **Under-Commercialized High-Margin Gems in Sugar:** Products in the Sugar division, such as *Everlasting Gobstopper* (80% margin, $8.00 profit/unit) and *Hair Toffee* (77.8% margin, $3.50 profit/unit), deliver exceptional unit economics but suffer from negligible sales volume ($427.48 total division revenue).

### Immediate 4-Step Executive Action Plan

#### 1. Renegotiate Supplier Terms for Kazookles (Timeline: 30 Days)
- **Problem:** Unit COGS is $3.00 against an invoice price of $3.25 (92.31% cost ratio).
- **Target:** Require supplier to reduce wholesale unit cost to $\le \$2.10$ (30% reduction).
- **Contingency:** If the supplier refuses, immediately raise wholesale price to $\$3.85$ or replace with higher-margin private-label chewable confectionery.
- **Projected Impact:** **+$350 to +$500 in direct profit recovery**.

#### 2. Implement Selective Repricing on Chocolate Volume (Timeline: 60 Days)
- **Rationale:** Confectionery demand exhibits low price elasticity ($\epsilon_d \approx -0.3$).
- **Action:** Apply a modest **+3.5% price increase** across the top 3 Wonka Bar lines (*Scrumdiddlyumptious, Triple Dazzle Caramel, Milk Chocolate*).
- **Projected Impact:** **+$2,850.00 annual gross profit uplift** with negligible volume attrition ($<1.0\%$).

#### 3. Rationalize Tail SKUs in Sugar Division (Timeline: 90 Days)
- **Problem:** *Fun Dip*, *Nerds*, and *Laffy Taffy* combined generate less than $85 in gross profit across two entire years, consuming disproportionate administrative and warehouse slotting overhead.
- **Action:** Set a minimum order threshold of 100 units per customer account for low-tier Sugar SKUs; discontinue unviable SKUs by Q1 2027.

#### 4. Scale High-Margin Niche Assets via B2B Cross-Merchandising (Timeline: 90 Days)
- **Action:** Create "Wonka Golden Bundle" promotions pairing *Everlasting Gobstoppers* (80% margin, $8.00 unit profit) with Wonka Chocolate orders.
- **Target:** Expand Gobstopper volume from 13 units to 500 units annually.
- **Projected Impact:** **+$3,900.00 in high-margin gross profit**.
